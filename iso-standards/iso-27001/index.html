ISO 1234:2022 Certification

Protect your data and information assets with Information Security Management System certification

What is ISO 1234?

ISO 1234:2022 is an international standard for Information Security Management Systems (ISMS). It provides a systematic approach to managing sensitive company information so that it remains secure.

Benefits

  • Protection of sensitive information
  • Reduced risk of data breaches
  • Compliance with data protection regulations
  • Enhanced customer trust
  • Competitive advantage

Key Requirements

  • Information security policy
  • Risk assessment and treatment
  • Access control
  • Cryptographic controls
  • Incident management
  • Business continuity planning
  • Management review

Certification Process

Our ISO 1234 certification process includes information security risk assessment, documentation support, implementation guidance, internal audits, and final certification audit. We help you secure your information assets.
