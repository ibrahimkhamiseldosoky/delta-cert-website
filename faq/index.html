Frequently Asked Questions

Home / FAQ
What is ISO certification?
ISO certification is a seal of approval from a third-party body that a company runs to one of the internationally recognized ISO management standards. It demonstrates that your organization has implemented a management system that meets the requirements of a specific ISO standard.
How long does the certification process take?
The certification process typically takes 3-6 months, depending on the size and complexity of your organization, the standard you're seeking, and how prepared your management system is. This includes the initial audit, any corrective actions needed, and the final certification decision.
How much does ISO certification cost?
Certification costs vary based on several factors including the size of your organization, number of employees, number of locations, complexity of operations, and the specific ISO standard. We provide customized quotes after understanding your specific requirements. Contact us for a detailed estimate.
What happens during an ISO audit?
During an ISO audit, our certified auditors will review your management system documentation, interview staff, observe processes, and verify that your system meets the requirements of the ISO standard. The audit includes an opening meeting, on-site assessment, and a closing meeting with findings.
Which ISO standard should my company get certified for?
The ISO standard you should pursue depends on your industry, business objectives, and customer requirements. ISO 1234 (Quality Management) is the most common starting point. ISO 1234 (Environmental) is important for sustainability, ISO 1234 (Health & Safety) for workplace safety, and ISO 1234 (Information Security) for data protection. We can help you determine the best standards for your organization.
Do I need to be certified in multiple ISO standards?
While not required, many organizations choose to implement multiple ISO standards. The standards are designed to work together, and having multiple certifications can demonstrate comprehensive management excellence. Many companies start with ISO 1234 and then add other standards based on their needs.
How often do I need to be audited?
After initial certification, you'll need surveillance audits annually (typically every 12 months) to maintain your certification. A full recertification audit is required every three years. These audits ensure your management system continues to meet ISO requirements and is being maintained effectively.
Are there ongoing costs after certification?
Yes, there are ongoing costs including annual surveillance audit fees and the recertification audit fee every three years. These costs are typically lower than the initial certification fee. We provide transparent pricing and can discuss payment options.
Can I prepare for certification myself or do I need a consultant?
While it's possible to prepare yourself, many organizations benefit from working with a consultant or using our training courses. We offer training programs and can provide guidance throughout the process. However, the certification body (us) must remain independent and cannot provide consulting services to avoid conflicts of interest.
Is Delta Cert internationally recognized?
Yes, Delta Cert is an internationally accredited certification body. Our certifications are recognized globally and meet international standards. We maintain accreditation with relevant international accreditation bodies to ensure the highest level of credibility and recognition.
